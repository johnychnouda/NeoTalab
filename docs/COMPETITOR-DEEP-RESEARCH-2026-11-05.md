# Competing with Toters, Noknok and the delivery world: deeper research (5 November 2026)

Desk research. Most sources are vendor blogs that sell direct-ordering tools, so every number below is an estimate unless it says otherwise. Nothing here was tested in the apps. Extends `COMPETITOR-NOTES-2026-11-05.md`.

## 1. The two companies are different kinds of business

| | Toters | Noknok |
|---|---|---|
| Model | Marketplace: takes a commission from restaurants and shops, adds delivery fees | Not a marketplace: its own warehouses and 80+ distributors, delivers groceries itself |
| Commission from shops | Reported 20 to 25% (older Daraj report), 22 to 30% (2026 guides, vendor source); no official rate card found | None: it sells its own stock |
| Money | Series B in 2022, reported as $15M or $18M (sources differ); about 1,279 staff (Tracxn, Sep 2026) | Funding unclear: one source says none, another says it was acquired by Gray Mackenzie Retail Lebanon in July 2024; revenue estimates $1M to $11.6M, unaudited |
| Strengths | Brand, app users, huge shop choice, own rider network, payments | Fast delivery (15 to 20 minutes claimed), reliable stock, flat fee (109,990 LBP) |
| Weaknesses (anecdotal) | High fees, wrong orders, slow support, rider-pay disputes | Smaller choice, own stock only, limited area |

Other players: Talabat came back to Lebanon in 2024 (smaller than Toters, 22 to 30% commission, vendor source); Deliveroo left in 2022; Uber Eats is not present.

**Key point:** Noknok does not compete with NeoTalab for restaurants at all. Toters and Talabat are the real rivals for restaurants, and their weak point is the commission.

## 2. Where a restaurant's money goes

- Marketplace commission: about 20 to 30% of each order (Toters and Talabat in Lebanon; Uber Eats and DoorDash 15 to 30% in the US, real blended cost 25 to 35% with extras).
- Own channel: payment cost 2 to 3% plus own rider about $2 to $5 per delivery (vendor estimate).
- For a $15 order: a marketplace takes about $3 to $4.50. NeoTalab's Meta cost is about 9.5 cents at best (see `MESSAGE-STUDY-2026-11-05.md`).

## 3. WhatsApp in Lebanon

- No independent figure exists for how many Lebanese orders come through WhatsApp. Sources only say WhatsApp and Instagram are central to small-business sales.
- Plain WhatsApp ordering by hand stops working past 30 to 40 orders a day (vendor claim). This is the gap NeoTalab fills by answering automatically.
- Cash on delivery is reported at 60 to 75% of delivery orders. No source covered Whish or the lira/dollar split.
- Elsewhere (Brazil, India) WhatsApp ordering is mainstream. A Brazilian survey (Abrasel 2024, as quoted by a vendor) found 63% of restaurants sell through WhatsApp, but it drove 26% of delivery revenue against 54% for marketplace apps. Marketplaces still win on discovery.

## 4. Where NeoTalab can win, and where it cannot (yet)

**Wins**
1. No commission. The shop pays a subscription and Meta's small fee. At 100 orders a day the difference is thousands of dollars a month.
2. The shop owns the customer and their number, so it can remarket. Marketplaces do not share this.
3. Works where customers already are (WhatsApp), with no app to install.
4. The customer can type, send a voice note or pick from a form, and the order still lands in one clean place.
5. Arabic, French and English, lira and dollar, cash, Whish: built for Lebanon.

**Gaps (they have it, we do not or have not proven it)**
1. **Discovery.** Toters brings new customers. NeoTalab brings the shop's own customers. We need a way for new customers to find the shop's WhatsApp number (QR codes, Instagram, Google Maps link, "Message us" ads). That is also what makes Meta's free ad window apply.
2. **Riders.** Toters has its own fleet. NeoTalab shops use their own drivers. Not researched here: whether NeoTalab can offer a rider hand-off.
3. **Saved details and reorder.** Both competitors have "same as last time". Cheap to add (one message), strong for regulars.
4. **Suggest a similar item** when something is sold out. Today we only say it is sold out.
5. **Live order tracking and support.** Toters is criticised for slow support; this is a chance, not a gap, if the shop's accept and status messages stay fast.
6. **Proof at scale.** Our load tests are on a lab. Real shops, real WhatsApp delivery and real payments are not yet tested.

## 5. What "strongest" means in practice

Not copying Toters. NeoTalab is "your own Toters inside WhatsApp, without the commission". Order of work to get there:

1. Message counter and usage card (measure the Meta cost honestly).
2. Typed whole-order step (the biggest cost cut).
3. Reorder ("same as last time") and saved address.
4. Similar-item suggestion for sold-out items.
5. Catalog or form cart for big menus.
6. Discovery kit for shops: QR code, link, Instagram button wording.
