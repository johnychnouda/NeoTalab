# Toters and Noknok: what they do, and what NeoTalab can learn (5 November 2026)

Desk research only: app-store listings, the companies' own pages and press. Nothing here was tested in the apps, and reviews are anecdotes. The apps change often; check them before copying anything.

## What they are

| | Toters | Noknok |
|---|---|---|
| Kind | Lebanese delivery "super app" (food, groceries, parcels, a bike "Butler" errand service) | Lebanese grocery delivery app, relaunched in October 2025 as an "everything" app |
| How customers order | Their own mobile app | Their own mobile app, live chat for support |
| WhatsApp ordering | Not found | Not found |

## Cart and checkout features they offer (from their own release notes and pages)

| Feature | Toters | Noknok |
|---|---|---|
| Saved addresses and payment | Yes | Not stated |
| Reorder a past order | Reported, not confirmed | Barcode scan and "save for later" lists |
| Scheduled delivery | Yes | Weekly scheduled orders |
| Favourite shops | Yes | Not stated |
| Suggestions at checkout | Yes ("complete your cart") | Not stated |
| Out-of-stock item: offer a similar one | Yes | Not stated |
| One cart across categories | Not stated | Yes |
| Order breakdown before paying | Yes, clearer in a later release | Not stated |

## What customers say (anecdotal)

- Toters: complaints about fees (a small-order fee, a delivery fee on small orders), wrong or missing items, slow support. Drivers' pay disputes made the news.
- Noknok: reviews in the app store are mostly good (fast, accurate), but I found no independent source.
- Noknok's own FAQ gives a flat delivery fee of 109,990 LBP and a 15 to 20 minute delivery time. This may be out of date.

## What NeoTalab can learn

1. **Customers already know the "fill a cart, send once" pattern.** The typed whole-order step and the later catalog cart or form match it.
2. **Reorder is the feature they both have.** For a restaurant with regulars, "same as last time" is a strong shortcut and costs one message. Worth adding after the first steps.
3. **Order breakdown before paying.** Our order summary with the total already does this.
4. **Offer a similar item when something is sold out.** We currently say it is sold out (D46). Suggesting an alternative is a later improvement.
5. **Fees and wrong orders are what people complain about.** Our summary-before-confirm step and the shop's accept step address both.
